The main drives go in one direction. The short spurs are PR-191, Juana Díaz on Reyes morning and the coastal headlands. Ceiba keeps the ferry and mainland backup close.

Use daylight for beaches, forest and cliffs. Dinner, the bio bay and Thursday museum galleries work after dark. If behind, cut Condado Lagoon, the salt flats or Guajataca first. Friday picks up the museum if Thursday runs late.

Is Culebra worth the ferry?

Yes, for Flamenco’s standout white-sand bay — if the group wants a beach-focused island day. Expect about 2½ hours at the beach. The mainland day also works with the same Ceiba stay.

Two ferry crossings total, plus two short road-van rides. No water-taxi hopping. Published Monday Jan 4 departures are 9am out and 3:30pm back; that timetable does not prove seats are released or available.

Budget $40–50 each for ferry, vans, beach entry and shared parking, excluding food. The old ~$5 return fare is not a safe Jan 2027 assumption: a visitor fare of $11.25 each way was reported to take effect Dec 31. Confirm the actual January price at checkout.

Plan to reach each terminal 60–75min early. The ferry lists sales as final. Choose Culebra only if both tickets are available with the 3:30 return for the 8pm kayak. Eat lunch at Flamenco and take the 1:30 return van.

Mainland fallback: slow breakfast → Playa Los Machos for a picnic and beach walk if open → Las Croabas waterfront → evening bio bay. Use Seven Seas if public beach access is open. Café Playero is a lunch option here.
